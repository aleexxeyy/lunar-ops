01 // MISSION BRIEFING

Learning the trade-offs of survival

THE PROBLEM

Space lessons for kids are either flat facts on a poster or too technical to follow. Students see how a mission ends — never the decisions behind it.

OUR MISSION

Lunar Ops puts the player in command of Horizon base on the Moon. Keep three astronauts safe, finish the science program — and learn that no choice is free.
